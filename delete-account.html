Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

Open Easy Save → Settings → Delete my data → confirm. This permanently deletes your cloud budget, savings decisions, questions, and feedback, signs you out, and wipes the phone. Done immediately.

Email 1ogbonnaeze@gmail.com from your sign-in address with the subject “Delete my Easy Save account”. We delete everything described above within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Budget, savings decisions, reflection questions, feedback messages, and the sign-in association. Analytics events already sent cannot identify you after sign-out and expire within 12 months. We never freeze accounts instead of deleting: deletion means deletion.
