Privacy Policy — Easy Save

App: Easy Save  ·  Developer: Easy Save (individual developer)  ·  Contact: 1ogbonnaeze@gmail.com  ·  Effective date: 8 October 2026

Easy Save helps you pause before impulse spending: it shows a Reality-Check question over your bank app's Pay screens and tracks a monthly save budget. This policy explains what data the app accesses, collects, uses, and shares. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. Data we never collect

No location, no contacts, no call/SMS data, no camera, no microphone, no health data, no inventory of other apps beyond detecting the supported bank apps listed in Settings, and no persistent device identifiers (IMEI, IMSI, SIM serial) linked to your data. No advertising ID is collected or linked to anything.

3. Permissions and what they do

  • Display over other apps — draws the Reality-Check question over the bank app's Pay screen.
  • Accessibility (Easy Save service) — detects Pay / Send / Transfer taps in the supported bank apps and reads the amount shown so the question can appear. The service description is shown in Android settings before you enable it, and the same explanation appears in the app's Setup screens before any permission is requested.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — keeps detection running so the question keeps appearing.
  • Internet — sign-in, backup, analytics. All traffic uses HTTPS.

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database + sign-in), hosted in the EU (eu-west-1) — stores your account, budget, decisions, questions, and feedback. Your data is isolated per account: the app can only read and write rows belonging to the signed-in user.
  • PostHog (product analytics), EU-hosted — receives usage events. Amounts, emails, codes, passwords, and similar values are stripped on-device before sending; session replay is off.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in (handled by Google's own sign-in screen under Google's privacy policy).

We do not sell personal data to anyone. We do not show ads and share nothing with advertisers. Data is disclosed only to the processors above to run the app, or if required by law with notice to you.

5. Children

Easy Save is a personal finance tool intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

6. Security

All network traffic uses modern encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud data is protected by per-account access rules so one account can never read another's. Analytics payloads are scrubbed of sensitive values on-device before sending. No method is perfect, but we limit collection to what the app needs and protect it in transit and at rest.

7. Retention

Your budget, decisions, questions, and feedback are kept until you delete them: use Settings → Delete my data in the app, or email a deletion request (see §8). Analytics events retained by PostHog are kept up to 12 months and are not linked to your identity after you sign out. Where law requires short retention for security or fraud prevention, we disclose it here and keep the minimum.

8. Your rights: access, export, deletion

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my data permanently deletes your cloud budget, decisions, questions, and feedback, signs you out, and wipes the phone. This is the fastest way.
  • By email: write to 1ogbonnaeze@gmail.com from your sign-in address with the subject “Delete my Easy Save account”. We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by reply. Account freezing is never a substitute: deletion means deletion.
  • A dedicated deletion guide is always available at ogbdecent.github.io/easysave-legal/delete-account.html with no sign-in required.

9. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the Effective date above is updated and the new version is posted at this same address before the change takes effect. Material changes are also announced in the app.
